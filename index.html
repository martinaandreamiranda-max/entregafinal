Meet Sabrina

Who is Sabrina Carpenter?

Sabrina Carpenter is an American singer, songwriter and actress who has grown from a young Disney Channel star into one of pop music's most recognizable voices.

Known for her playful personality, witty lyrics and distinctive style, Sabrina has continuously reinvented herself throughout her career. From her early pop beginnings to the confidence and glamour of her latest releases, each chapter reveals a new side of her artistry.
